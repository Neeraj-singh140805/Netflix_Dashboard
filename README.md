## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix's content catalog to identify trends and patterns in movies and TV shows.

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Content distribution between Movies and TV Shows
- Netflix content ratings
- Release trends over the years
- Genre distribution
- Country-wise content availability
- Most common genres
- Content growth over time

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **8,778+ Netflix titles** were analyzed.
- **69.6%** of the content consists of Movies.
- **30.4%** of the content consists of TV Shows.
- **TV-MA** is the dominant content rating.
- Netflix experienced significant growth in content releases over the years.
- **2018** was one of the peak years for content releases.
